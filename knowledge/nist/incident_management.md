# Incident Response Risk Management

## Attack overview
Incident response should be integrated into organizational cybersecurity risk management. Preparation, detection, response, recovery, and continual improvement depend on clear ownership.

## Indicators
Unassigned alerts, delayed escalation, missing procedures, unclear authority, and untested recovery processes.

## Detection
Review incident intake, escalation records, readiness exercises, and service recovery metrics. Assess gaps against organizational risk objectives.

## Mitigation
Define roles and communications, prepare evidence procedures, exercise playbooks, and integrate lessons into governance and protective controls.

## MITRE ID
No direct technique asserted. Require behavioral evidence before assigning ATT&CK.
