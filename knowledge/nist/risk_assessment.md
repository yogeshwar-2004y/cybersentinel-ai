# Incident Risk Assessment

## Attack overview
Incident prioritization combines evidence strength, likely scope, asset criticality, and business consequences. A heuristic numerical score is not a measured financial loss.

## Indicators
Critical-service involvement, privileged access, sensitive data, expanding scope, and operational disruption.

## Detection
Validate asset owners, dependencies, data classification, and affected users. Reassess severity as new evidence arrives.

## Mitigation
Document assumptions, uncertainty, owners, and escalation criteria. Align containment choices with business continuity needs.

## MITRE ID
No direct technique asserted. Require behavioral evidence before assigning ATT&CK.
