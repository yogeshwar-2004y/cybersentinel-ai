# Recovery Planning

## Attack overview
Recovery restores trusted operations while verifying that the initiating cause and persistence have been addressed. Fast restoration without validation may permit recurrence.

## Indicators
Unverified backups, repeated alerts after restoration, missing integrity checks, and incomplete service validation.

## Detection
Test backup integrity, compare configurations with trusted baselines, and monitor restored assets and dependent services.

## Mitigation
Use approved recovery criteria, staged restoration, accountable service owners, and post-restoration monitoring.

## MITRE ID
No direct technique asserted. Require behavioral evidence before assigning ATT&CK.
