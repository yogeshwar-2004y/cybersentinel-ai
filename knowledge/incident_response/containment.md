# Containment Decisions

## Attack overview
Containment limits harm while preserving evidence and sustaining important services. Restrictions can affect business operations and should be scoped and approved.

## Indicators
Ongoing suspicious sessions, expanding host scope, repeated outbound connections, and disrupted services.

## Detection
Determine affected assets, identities, dependencies, and current attacker activity. Record expected business effects and rollback options.

## Mitigation
Obtain accountable approval for host isolation, account restriction, and network changes; monitor outcomes and adapt the scope.

## MITRE ID
No direct technique asserted. Require behavioral evidence before assigning ATT&CK.
