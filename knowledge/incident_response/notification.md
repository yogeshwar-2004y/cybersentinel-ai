# Incident Communication

## Attack overview
Incident communication coordinates technical response and business decisions. Reports should separate confirmed evidence, hypotheses, and unknown impact.

## Indicators
Conflicting severity labels, unclear ownership, untracked actions, and unsupported impact claims.

## Detection
Review audience needs, escalation thresholds, action ownership, and the accuracy of reported facts. Protect sensitive incident details.

## Mitigation
Maintain an incident record, approved contact paths, clear update cadence, and a timeline of decisions and recovery status.

## MITRE ID
No direct technique asserted. Require behavioral evidence before assigning ATT&CK.
