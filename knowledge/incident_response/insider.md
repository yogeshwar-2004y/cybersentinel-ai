# Insider Threat Investigation

## Attack overview
An authorized person may misuse access, but unusual employee activity can also reflect legitimate work. Intent cannot be inferred from a volume anomaly.

## Indicators
Bulk export outside ordinary duties, unusual access to sensitive material, and unexplained destination changes.

## Detection
Compare role permissions, work tasks, approved transfers, and data ownership. Coordinate handling of employee-related evidence with authorized stakeholders.

## Mitigation
Apply least privilege, protect sensitive data, retain evidence, and coordinate proportional response with legal, HR, and security leadership.

## MITRE ID
No direct technique asserted. Require behavioral evidence before assigning ATT&CK.
