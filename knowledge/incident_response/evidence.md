# Evidence Preservation

## Attack overview
Investigations require a reliable record of what was collected, where it came from, and how it was handled. Poor preservation can obstruct root-cause analysis.

## Indicators
Missing timestamps, mutable logs, incomplete hashes, undocumented transfers, and ambiguous collection sources.

## Detection
Verify collection time, source system, file hashes, collector identity, and handling records. Maintain both originals and working copies.

## Mitigation
Follow approved collection procedures, record custody, restrict access, and protect integrity and retention.

## MITRE ID
No direct technique asserted. Require behavioral evidence before assigning ATT&CK.
