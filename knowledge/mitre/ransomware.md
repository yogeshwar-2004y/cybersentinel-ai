# Data Encrypted for Impact

## Attack overview
Encryption of business data can disrupt operations and support extortion. A malware family or operator cannot be identified from volume metrics alone.

## Indicators
Mass file rewrites, unusual extensions, inaccessible files, ransom notes, and related endpoint alerts.

## Detection
Correlate file modification bursts with process ancestry, affected shares, backup alerts, and independent malware evidence.

## Mitigation
Escalate to incident command, preserve evidence, approve containment, protect backups, and restore validated clean assets in stages.
