# Phishing

## Attack overview
Deceptive messages can prompt credential submission, link visits, or attachment execution. Message appearance alone is insufficient to establish downstream compromise.

## Indicators
Sender inconsistencies, unfamiliar destinations, suspicious attachments, and new sign-ins following user interaction.

## Detection
Preserve headers, URLs, delivery records, recipient scope, and endpoint/identity events. Use safe analysis workflows for attachments.

## Mitigation
Strengthen mail controls and MFA, train reporting behavior, and coordinate removal of confirmed malicious messages and session revocation.
